Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TelecelLoans, answered clearly.

What happens after I apply?
▼

After confirming the review step, you are redirected to verify.html with your application details for the verification step.

How long does the application take?
▼

The application form takes under 2 minutes. You only need your full name, Telecel number, employment status, and monthly income to continue.

Does my Telecel usage affect how much I can borrow?
▼

Your application details and selected amount help determine what should be reviewed during verification.

What is the maximum loan amount?
▼

GHS 1,000 to GHS 50,000 depending on your subscriber history, income level, and repayment track record. Limits grow automatically with consistent Telecel usage.

What is the interest rate?
▼

A flat 8.9% per month on the loan principal for each month of your chosen repayment period. All amounts are shown upfront in the calculator before you apply — no surprises.

Which Telecel numbers can I use?
▼

Any active Ghanaian Telecel number using the supported prefixes: 050, 020, 024, 054, 055, and 059. Enter your full 10-digit number when applying.

How do I repay my TelecelLoan?
▼

The repayment calculator shows your estimated monthly amount before you continue, so you can review the terms first.

What if I miss a repayment?
▼

Missing a repayment may reduce your future borrowing limit and incur a small late fee. Review the repayment amount carefully before continuing and contact support early if you foresee difficulties.
