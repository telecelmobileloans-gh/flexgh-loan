Who Qualifies

Built for Loyal Telecel Users

The more actively you use Telecel, the higher your loan limit. Consistent Telecel usage is your credit score here.

01
Active Telecel Subscriber

Must be an active Telecel subscriber with regular usage — calls, data, or Telecel Cash transactions. Longer subscriber history = bigger loan limit.

02
Telecel Cash Account

You need a registered, active Telecel Cash mobile money account. Funds are disbursed directly and repayments auto-deducted from it.

03
Age 18+ & Income Source

Must be at least 18 years old with a verifiable income. Employed, self-employed, business owners, and traders all qualify.

04
Regular Telecel Activity

Telecel monitors your usage patterns to set your limit. Regular airtime top-ups, data bundles, and Telecel Cash transactions boost your eligible amount.

05
No Active Default

Must not have a defaulted TelecelLoans repayment. Good repayment history on previous loans increases your limit with every cycle.

06
Ghana Resident

Available exclusively to residents of Ghana using a Ghanaian Telecel SIM registered under your valid national ID.

Got Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does Telecel loan approval take?
▼
Most active Telecel subscribers are approved within 15 minutes. The system checks your Telecel usage history and eligibility in real-time, 24/7 including weekends and public holidays.
Who is eligible for TelecelLoans?
▼
Active Telecel subscribers aged 18+ with a registered Telecel Cash account and a verifiable income. The longer and more actively you've used Telecel — calls, data, Telecel Cash — the higher your eligible loan amount.
Does my Telecel usage affect how much I can borrow?
▼
Yes, directly. Regular Telecel activity — consistent airtime top-ups, active data usage, and Telecel Cash transactions — increases your eligible loan limit. Think of your Telecel activity as your credit history with us.
What is the maximum loan amount?
▼
GHS 1,000 to GHS 50,000 depending on your Telecel subscriber history, income, and repayment track record. First-time borrowers may start lower — limits grow automatically with good repayment behaviour.
What is the interest rate?
▼
A flat 8.9% per month applied to the principal for each month of your chosen repayment period. All amounts are shown upfront in our calculator before you apply — no surprises.
How do I repay my TelecelLoan?
▼
Repayment is automatic — deducted from your Telecel Cash account on the due date. You can also make early repayments anytime without any penalty fee. Everything is trackable from your dashboard.
What if I miss a repayment?
▼
Missing a repayment may affect your future borrowing limit and incur a small late fee. Ensure sufficient funds in your Telecel Cash account before the due date. Contact Telecel support early if you anticipate difficulty.
