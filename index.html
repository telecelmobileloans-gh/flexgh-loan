❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply? +
Any active Telecel Ghana subscriber with a registered Telecel Cash wallet and valid ID.
What is the maximum amount? +
You can borrow up to GHS 50,000 depending on your credit limit and usage history.
How long does it take? +
The application takes 2 minutes, and funds are sent instantly to your wallet.
Are there hidden fees? +
No. All costs are shown upfront in the calculator breakdown before you apply.
How do I repay? +
Repayment is automatically deducted from your Telecel Cash wallet on the due date.
Can I pay early? +
Yes, early repayment is allowed and may improve your credit score with us.
What if I pay late? +
Late payments may attract a penalty fee and affect your future loan eligibility.
Do I need collateral? +
No collateral is required for TelecelLoans. Your Telecel Cash account is your guarantee.
Is my data safe? +
Yes, we use bank-grade encryption and comply with all data protection regulations.
Can I have multiple loans? +
You can only have one active loan at a time. Once you repay, you can apply again.
