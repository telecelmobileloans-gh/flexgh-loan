Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TelecelLoans, answered clearly.

How long does approval take?
▼

Most active Telecel subscribers are approved within 15 minutes. Our automated system checks eligibility in real-time, 24/7 including weekends and public holidays — no human wait required.

Who is eligible for TelecelLoans?
▼

Active Telecel subscribers aged 18+ with a registered Telecel Cash account and verifiable income. The more actively you use Telecel — calls, data, Telecel Cash — the higher your eligible loan amount.

Does my Telecel usage affect how much I can borrow?
▼

Yes, directly. Regular activity — airtime top-ups, data bundles, and Telecel Cash transactions — increases your eligible limit automatically. Your usage history is your credit profile with us.

What is the maximum loan amount?
▼

GHS 1,000 to GHS 50,000 depending on your subscriber history, income level, and repayment track record. Limits grow automatically with consistent Telecel usage and on-time repayments.

What is the interest rate?
▼

A flat 8.9% per month on the loan principal for each month of your chosen repayment period. All amounts are shown upfront in the calculator before you apply — no surprises at repayment time.

Which Telecel numbers can I use?
▼

Any active Ghanaian Telecel number — including 050, 020, 024, 054, 055, and 059 prefixes. Your number must be registered under a valid Ghana Card or Voter ID and have an active Telecel Cash wallet.

How do I repay my TelecelLoan?
▼

Repayment is automatic — deducted from your Telecel Cash account on the monthly due date. You can also repay early at any time with no penalty fee. Track everything on your account dashboard.

What if I miss a repayment?
▼

Missing a repayment may reduce your future borrowing limit and incur a small late fee. Ensure sufficient funds in your Telecel Cash before the due date. Contact support early if you foresee difficulties.
