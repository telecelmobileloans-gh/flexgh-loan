Got Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does approval take?
▼

Most active Telecel subscribers are approved within 15 minutes. Our system checks eligibility in real-time, 24/7 including weekends — fully automated, no human wait.

Who is eligible for TelecelLoans?
▼

Active Telecel subscribers aged 18+ with a registered Telecel Cash account and verifiable income. The more actively you use Telecel, the higher your eligible loan amount.

Does my Telecel usage affect how much I can borrow?
▼

Yes, directly. Regular Telecel activity — airtime top-ups, active data usage, and Telecel Cash transactions — increases your eligible loan limit. Your usage history is your credit profile.

What is the maximum loan amount?
▼

GHS 1,000 to GHS 50,000 depending on your Telecel subscriber history, income, and repayment track record. Limits grow automatically with good repayment behaviour.

What is the interest rate?
▼

A flat 8.9% per month on the principal for each month of your chosen repayment period. All amounts are shown upfront in the calculator — no surprises at repayment time.

How do I repay my TelecelLoan?
▼

Repayment is automatic — deducted from your Telecel Cash account on the due date. You can also repay early anytime with no penalty fee. Everything is trackable on your dashboard.

What if I miss a repayment?
▼

Missing a repayment may affect your future borrowing limit and incur a small late fee. Ensure sufficient funds in your Telecel Cash account before the due date. Contact Telecel support early if needed.
