❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply for TelecelLoans? +
Only active Telecel Ghana subscribers with a registered Telecel Cash wallet and valid ID can apply. Your Telecel usage history determines your eligibility.
What is the minimum and maximum loan amount? +
You can borrow from GHS 500 to GHS 50,000 depending on your credit limit, Telecel usage history, and account activity.
How long does the application process take? +
The application takes just 2 minutes to complete, and funds are disbursed instantly to your wallet.
Are there any hidden fees? +
No hidden fees. All costs including interest are shown upfront in the calculator before you apply.
How do I repay my loan? +
Repayment is automatically deducted from your Telecel Cash wallet on the due date you selected.
Can I pay back my loan early? +
Yes, early repayment is allowed and may improve your credit score with TelecelLoans.
What happens if I pay late? +
Late payments may attract a penalty fee and can affect your eligibility for future loans.
Do I need collateral to get a loan? +
No collateral is required. Your Telecel Cash account serves as your guarantee.
Is my personal data safe with TelecelLoans? +
Yes, we use bank-grade encryption and comply with all data protection regulations to keep your data safe.
Can I have multiple active loans at the same time? +
You can only have one active loan at a time. Once you repay, you can apply for another loan.
