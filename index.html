Got Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does approval take?
▼
Most applications are approved within 15 minutes. Our system checks eligibility in real-time, 24/7 including weekends and public holidays.
Who is eligible for TelecelLoans?
▼
Active Telecel subscribers aged 18+ with a registered Telecel Cash account and a verifiable income source. The longer your subscriber history, the higher your eligible amount.
What is the maximum loan amount?
▼
GHS 1,000 to GHS 50,000 depending on your subscriber history, income, and credit profile. First-time borrowers may start lower — limits increase with good repayment behaviour.
What is the interest rate?
▼
A flat 8.9% per month applied to the principal for each month of your chosen repayment period. All amounts shown upfront in the calculator — no surprises at repayment time.
How do I repay my loan?
▼
Repayment is automatic — the total amount is deducted from your Telecel Cash account on the due date. You can also make early repayments any time without any penalty fee.
Is my personal data secure?
▼
Yes. We use 256-bit bank-level encryption and strict security protocols. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties without your explicit consent.
What happens if I miss a repayment?
▼
Missing a repayment may affect your future borrowing limit and incur a small late fee. Ensure sufficient funds in your Telecel Cash account before the due date. Contact support if you anticipate difficulty.
